# Privacy Policy for TickAhead Track Countdowns

**Last updated:** October 2026

TickAhead ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
TickAhead Track Countdowns does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. All countdown data, event titles, dates, and notes created within the app are stored locally on your device.

### 2. Third-Party Services
TickAhead Track Countdowns does not share your data with third parties or use external analytics trackers.

### 3. Data Security
We value your trust in using our app. Since all data remains locally on your device, your event information is completely safe and private under your control.

### 4. Children's Privacy
TickAhead Track Countdowns does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
